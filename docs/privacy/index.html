🔒 Privacy and Data Protection

🛡️ How your data is protected

Audit Differently takes the security of your personal information seriously. We use a combination of technical, physical, and administrative safeguards to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, destruction, or alteration.

  • 🔐 Encryption: All personal data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard encryption.
  • 🔑 Access controls: Only authorized personnel with a strict business need have access to your data.
  • 🧹 Data minimization: We only collect and retain the personal data necessary to provide our services, and we enable you to securely delete it when it is no longer needed.
  • 🤝 Third-party security: Any third-party service providers or vendors we use are thoroughly vetted and contractually required to maintain high standards of data protection.

While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. If you have questions about our security practices, you can contact us using the information below.

📜 Privacy Statement

Audit Differently is committed to protecting your privacy and provided personal information.

We collect personal information, such as your name, email address, and other relevant details, to provide our services, respond to inquiries, and improve your experience.

We only use your information for legitimate business purposes and do not sell your personal data. We may share information with trusted service providers when necessary to deliver our services or comply with legal obligations.

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information and retain it only for as long as necessary.

Where applicable, you have the right to access, correct, or request the deletion of your personal data, restrict or object to certain processing, and withdraw consent. You may exercise these rights by contacting us or any organizational admin who manages your audit data in our tool.

For questions about this Privacy Statement or how we handle your personal data, contact us using the information below.

🗂️ Records and retention

Your organization is responsible for the retention of its own records, and for keeping its own copies of everything it must keep to satisfy legal or regulatory requirements. Audit Differently is not liable for data retention.

When an owner deletes an engagement or a workspace, it is permanent. We provide a full export first, which the owner must download before the deletion can go ahead. We keep a safety copy of that export for 30 days, only to cover an accidental delete or a failed download, and then it is destroyed.

🤖 How we use AI

Audit Differently can use an AI model for two jobs, and only when someone says yes. Each time, it fills in fields on your screen, and you check them before anything is saved. Without that yes, nothing goes to an AI model, and you map or type the fields yourself.

  • Importing transactions: when you press "Suggest the mapping with AI", we send the file's column names (for example "Date", "Name", "Amount") to suggest which column holds what. We never send a row of the file.
  • Uploading receipts: when you tick "Read the amount, date and vendor with AI", we send those receipt files to read the amount, date and vendor whenever a PDF's text does not give all three. The box is unticked every time you upload.
  • Emailed receipts: nobody is there to ask when a receipt is emailed in, so a workspace owner decides. It is off unless an owner turns on "Read emailed receipts with AI" in the workspace's settings.

We never send personal documents (eligibility records, enrollment forms, pay stubs) to an AI model, whatever is ticked or turned on. When AI read a receipt or suggested a mapping, the workspace's activity log says so.

The AI model is run by a service provider that processes what we send on our behalf, under a contract with us, to give back the answer. We use what comes back only to fill in those fields.

🧩 Services we use

Besides the AI provider above, we use service providers to host the app and its database, to store your documents, and to sign you in. Each handles your data only to provide that service to us.

Email: receipts emailed to your workspace's receipts address are received for us by an email service provider, which passes the message and its attachments to the app. The same provider delivers what you send us through the contact form. The app never sends a document by email.
